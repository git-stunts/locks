## 2b. Proposed Solution

## 2c. Alternatives considered and rejected

Use a documented offline command if safe online maintenance is not established.

## 2d. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Implement the retention and quiescence rules from GL-011.
- [ ] Preserve live authority, required snapshots, and writer objects.
- [ ] Report loose, packed, reachable, and retained data with a clear measurement basis.
- [ ] Show bounded object growth after repeated lifecycle and maintenance cycles.

## 2e. Test Plan

Golden: Maintain a healthy store and retain its authority.
Edges: Cover pinned readers, failed writers, and empty stores.
Known failure modes: Interrupt maintenance and verify recoverability.
Fuzz and stress: Measure a bounded churn pilot before a larger campaign.
